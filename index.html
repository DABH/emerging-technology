The course

Theory, then practice.

Offered through Vanderbilt’s School of Engineering as ENGM 3890 for undergraduates and ENGM 5890 for graduate students, the course examines how emerging technologies are invented, funded, and brought to market, and what they mean for society along the way.

One technology at a time

Each unit takes on a single technology and covers how it works, where it is headed, and its social, environmental, and ethical implications.

Practitioners in the room

About once a week, a guest speaker joins the class, from venture capitalists and executives to founders and engineers. Some sessions move off campus, to places like Nashville Electric Service and the Wond’ry.

A venture of your own

Student teams find a real pain point, run customer discovery, size the market, and finish the semester with a pitch and demo of a commercializable idea built on at least one course technology.
